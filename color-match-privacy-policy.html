Privacy Policy — Color Match QC

Last updated: September 2026

Color Match QC ("the app") is developed by Kryczka Studios Universe ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app processes and why.

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not require account creation or sign-in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any personal identifying information.
  • Photos taken for color measurement are processed entirely on your device. They are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on our servers — we do not operate a server that receives your photos or measurement data at all.

Data stored locally on your device

The app saves the following information locally on your phone only, using standard Android app storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us:

  • Your saved color standards library
  • Your measurement history and reports
  • Your selected app language

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data. We have no way to access, recover, or view it.

Advertising

The app displays ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect data such as your advertising identifier, general device information, and approximate location derived from your IP address. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. You can review how Google handles this data, and manage your ad personalization preferences, here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Ad Settings

Permissions the app requests

  • Camera — used only to photograph a color sample for measurement. Images are processed locally and are not saved as photo files or transmitted anywhere.
  • Internet — required to display ads and is not used to transmit your measurement data.

Children's privacy

This app is a professional color quality-control tool intended for adult users in industrial, print, and paint-related trades. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights (EU / EEA users)

Since we do not collect or store any personal data on our own servers, there is no personal data held by us to access, correct, or delete. Any data-related rights regarding advertising data are managed directly through Google's own privacy tools linked above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
