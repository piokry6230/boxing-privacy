1. Who we are

MMA Mentor King ("the game") is developed by Kryczka Studios Universe (KSU), an independent developer. Contact: kryczka.dev@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal data. The game works offline. Your progress (saves, settings) is stored only on your device and never sent to us. We have no user accounts and no servers that receive your data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads and occasional full-screen ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad-interaction data. Where required (UK / EEA), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time with the "Ad privacy settings" button in the Bonuses tab. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. App updates

The game may use Google Play's In-App Updates service to tell you when a new version is available. This is handled by Google Play.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Deleting your data

Because all game data is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available at this address.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy: kryczka.dev@gmail.com
