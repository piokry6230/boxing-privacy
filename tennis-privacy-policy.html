1. Overview

Tennis Manager King ("the App") is an offline tennis management game. We (Kryczka Studios) do not operate any servers for this App, do not require an account and do not collect, store or transmit any personal data to ourselves.

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (up to 3 save slots), settings such as language and sound, and in-game names you type in (manager, team, player names) are stored only locally on your device. They are never sent to us. You can delete this data at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

3. Advertising – Google AdMob

The App shows ads (optional rewarded ads and occasional full-screen ads) provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. This data is processed by Google, not by us, in accordance with Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

The App declares the Android "Advertising ID" (AD_ID) permission for this purpose.

4. Consent (UK and EEA users)

If you are in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are shown. You can accept or decline. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Extras → Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads).

5. App updates and links

The App may use Google Play's in-app update feature to tell you when a new version is available; this check is handled by Google Play on your device. The "All KSU productions" screen contains links to our other apps on Google Play; opening them takes you to Google Play, which is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

Because we do not collect or transmit personal data, your game data is protected by your device's own security. Data processed by Google AdMob is protected by Google.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: kryczka.dev@gmail.com

Estos datos los trata Google, no nosotros, conforme a sus políticas:

  • Política de privacidad de Google
  • Cómo utiliza Google la información de sitios o aplicaciones que usan sus servicios
